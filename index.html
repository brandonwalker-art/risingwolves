🌟 Rising Wolves is a youth track team designed to build confidence, teach athletic principles, and work on individual improvement — all while having fun! 🎉 Two program options are available: Elementary Program and Competition Program.

Programs
🌱
Ages K – 6th Grade
Elementary Program

A 6-week program designed for elementary aged kids, K–6th, who are beginners or intermediates at track and field. This program teaches basic principles on running, jumping, and throwing. Practices are made fun, to get outside, get active, and to learn the basics of track and field.

Ages 10 – 15
Competition Program

An 11-week program designed for 10–15 year olds who are either new to track and field or want to further develop their skills. The program teaches foundational and advanced techniques to the sport. Practices are focused to not only learn the basics, but also to prepare the athlete for high school athletics.
